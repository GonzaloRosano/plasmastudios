## About me

Game Design and Development student at U-tad, with a well-rounded view of development (game design, 3D art, narrative and basic logic).

My main goal is Level Design, focusing on the player's spatial experience: from the initial conceptualization in top-down layouts to building the level through the Greybox / Blockout phase. I focus on defining sightlines, exploration pacing, intuitive navigation and visual guidance.

---

## What I do

- **Top-down planning and sketching**: designing top-down layouts, flow diagrams, combat zones, bottlenecks and alternative routes using Excalidraw
- **Greyboxing / 3D Blockout**: building and quickly prototyping levels at real scale in Unreal Engine and Unity to validate pacing and movement metrics
- **Player guidance**: spatial composition, landmarks, guiding lighting and focal points to naturally guide the player
