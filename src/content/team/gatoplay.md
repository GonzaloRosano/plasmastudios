## About me

Hi everyone. I'm **Gatoplay**, or just **Gato** for friends. I'm a YouTuber and streamer covering Nintendo content, mainly the Mario franchise. I also do video editing, pixel art, texture creation, voice impressions as a hobby, and I'm a Playmaker whenever I can be one.

I started making content in 2016, with a small YouTube channel that I ended up losing for personal reasons. In 2017 I started streaming as a hobby and, finally, in 2021 I started streaming more consistently on Twitch, where I still am today. I created my current YouTube account in 2019, though I didn't start using it actively until 2022, when I posted a Karmaland animation made entirely from my phone.

My interest in pixel art started in 2018, when I made my first pieces from my phone using an app called *PixelStation*. Over time I moved through tools like Paint until landing on **Paint Tool SAI**, which I now use for illustrations, pixel art and thumbnails for my videos.

Voice impressions started as a joke and are still mainly a hobby today — after years of practicing, I've perfected some to the point that people have told me they're pretty good.
